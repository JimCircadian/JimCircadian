I used to work at the [British Antarctic Survey](https://www.bas.ac.uk), so have a computational domain interest leaning towards the environmental sciences. Previously I worked for industry in various forms, but that's long behind me... :wink:

[My personal self-hosted website is called Inconsistent Records](https://inconsistentrecords.co.uk). I used to have a [professional(ish) profile over at BAS](https://www.bas.ac.uk/profile/jambyr). There is a more [community oriented profile over at the Software Sustainability Institute](https://www.software.ac.uk/about/fellows/james-byrne) or [my ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-3731-2377) for more research related bits I've been involved with.

Pretty soon I'll be looking at migrating new and old projects (that can stand it) off to [SourceHut](https://sr.ht). [There's good treatment of the arguments against GitHubs vendor lock practices at the Software Conservancy](https://sfconservancy.org/GiveUpGitHub/). **This will simply be about providing information for people to consider**, I won't compromise existing projects given the need to promote open source publications, even if that is being abused.
